# AutoMate.

**Shifts. Bills. Simplified.**

A clearer plan for shift work and everyday bills. See your rota, preparation times and the recorded bills due before payday. Built for iPhone and iPad.

Avara is becoming AutoMate. The redesigned experience is coming in version 1.2.0; the App Store may still show the earlier name until that update is released.
